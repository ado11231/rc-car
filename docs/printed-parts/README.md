# Printed Parts

Each folder has the part's drawing (PDF) and a picture of it. [all-parts.pdf](all-parts.pdf) has all 7 drawings in one file. Numbers are the print order.

| # | Part | What it does | Drawing |
|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | Chassis plate | The base everything bolts to. Has the hole the servo drops into, 4 posts for the main board and a wing for the on/off switch. | [PDF](01-chassis-plate/01-chassis-plate.pdf) |
| 02 | Steering beam | Bar under the front. The servo arm screws onto its middle and a front wheel bolts to each end. | [PDF](02-steering-beam/02-steering-beam.pdf) |
| 03 | Battery tray | Holds the battery over the rear motors. The post on its back corner holds the antenna. | [PDF](03-battery-tray/03-battery-tray.pdf) |
| 04 | Front bumper | The plow. Screws under the plate and onto the servo box. Print it face down. | [PDF](04-front-bumper/04-front-bumper.pdf) |
| 05 | Servo box | Black cover over the front of the plate. The servo pokes out the top. | [PDF](05-servo-box/05-servo-box.pdf) |
| 06 | Antenna | Just for looks. Pushes into the post on the battery tray. Print it lying flat. | [PDF](06-antenna/06-antenna.pdf) |
| 07 | Tow hook | Hook under the back of the plate, for pulling things. | [PDF](07-tow-hook/07-tow-hook.pdf) |
